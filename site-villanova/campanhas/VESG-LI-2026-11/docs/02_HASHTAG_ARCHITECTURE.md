# Hashtag architecture

Rule: **exactly 3 hashtags per post** (4 only on recap posts), placed on the last line, in this order: campaign → pillar → audience. No hashtags inside sentences. No more than 4 — LinkedIn reach does not scale with tag count and the executive reader reads tag stuffing as noise.

## Tier 1 — Campaign (on every post)
- `#SupplierEvidence` — the thread. Owned term; low competition; matches the site's main noun.

## Tier 2 — Pillar (matches the week)
| Pillar | Tag | Weeks |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence gap / method | `#SEMM`, `#Compliance`, `#Traceability` | W1, W4, W5 |
| CBAM | `#CBAM`, `#CarbonExposure` | W2 |
| EUDR | `#EUDR`, `#DueDiligence` | W3 |
| CSDDD / contracts | `#CSDDD`, `#ContractRisk` | W4 |
| CSRD | `#CSRD` | W4 |
| Scope 3 | `#Scope3` | W5 |
| Research anchor | `#Research` | Saturday recaps |

## Tier 3 — Audience (who should stop scrolling)
| Audience | EN tag | PT tag |
|---|---|---|
| Finance leadership | `#CFO`, `#Boardroom` | `#CFO` |
| Procurement / buyers | `#Procurement`, `#SupplyChainRisk` | — |
| Brazilian exporters | `#Exporters` | `#Exportadores`, `#ComércioExterior`, `#RiscoRegulatório`, `#Rastreabilidade` |

## Mapping by front
- Authority post: `#SupplierEvidence` + pillar + (`#Procurement` | `#CFO` | `#Boardroom` | `#SupplyChainRisk` | `#Compliance` | `#DueDiligence` | `#Traceability`).
- Sales EN: `#SupplierEvidence` + pillar + (`#Exporters` | `#CFO` | `#Procurement`).
- Sales PT: `#SupplierEvidence` + pillar + (`#Exportadores` | `#ComércioExterior` | `#RiscoRegulatório` | `#CFO`).

## Do not use
`#ESG`, `#Sustainability`, `#GreenBusiness`, `#NetZero`, `#Innovation` — generic, attract the wrong audience and dilute the regulatory positioning. `#Ecobraz` on Villanova ESG posts — different entity, different page.
