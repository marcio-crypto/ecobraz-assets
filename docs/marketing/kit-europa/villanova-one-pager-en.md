## Your Brazilian operation is compliant. Can it prove it?

**Villanova ESG** is a specialist consultancy on the **Brazil–EU compliance corridor**.
We turn what your Brazilian operation already does into **buyer-readable, audit-ready
evidence** — the documentation your European auditors, regulators and board can
actually read, trust and defend.

### The problem we solve

European due-diligence, carbon and data-protection rules (CSDDD, CBAM, EUDR, GDPR/LGPD,
Scope 3 reporting) push documentation duties down the supply chain. Market access is not
compliance evidence: goods clear customs while headquarters still cannot obtain from
Brazil the structured evidence they are accountable for.

### What makes us different

Global consultancies write reports. **We deliver the report together with the verified
operation that sustains it.**

- **Own execution arm in Brazil**: Ecobraz, a Brazilian NGO with 15+ years of electronics
  reverse logistics — collection, certified data destruction and environmentally
  correct destination, serving multinationals, diplomatic missions and major
  hospital networks.
- **Document management and audit trail**: we structure, validate and issue the
  destination, data-sanitisation and ESG documentation of your Brazilian operation,
  ready for your own third-party assurance.
- **Published methodology**: the Supplier Evidence Maturity Model (SEMM) — our open
  framework for measuring EU-buyer-readiness of Brazilian suppliers (Zenodo/DOI).

### Services

1. **Evidence gap assessment** of your Brazilian subsidiary or suppliers (SEMM-based).
2. **Managed compliance documentation**: e-waste destination, certified data
   destruction (LGPD/GDPR-aligned), Scope 3 destination evidence — executed by
   Ecobraz, documented by Villanova ESG.
3. **Social impact with evidence**: the *Adote um Bairro* programme — your company
   funds household e-waste collections in São Paulo and receives the full destination
   documentation and data in its own name.
